About

One person, an LLC, and work you can inspect.

Max Wahlgren · DevOfCodes LLC · San Diego, California

I'm Max Wahlgren, a software engineer based in San Diego. I spent seven years building internal tools and systems across enterprise operations, progressing from analyst to senior engineer, and I watched the same friction play out on every team past a certain size: the architectural decision that lived in a chat thread nobody could find, the new developer who spent weeks reconstructing context that existed somewhere but not anywhere retrievable, the software that worked right up until someone else had to maintain it.

DevOfCodes is my one-person software company. It is not a team, and this site doesn't pretend otherwise. It's where I share the products, tools and experiments I'm working on, and where you can hire me for focused contract development when one of them shows you something you need.

The way I work is the thing I'm most confident selling. Every project starts with a written scope and acceptance criteria that can be checked without reading code. Work lands in units, each merged with its tests and documentation. A status record is kept as I go, so what exists and what doesn't is never a matter of memory. I use AI coding tools heavily, and I use them under that process rather than instead of it: the tools draft, the process decides, and the record shows both.

You can see the results in the projects: a design-to-instructions tool for Minecraft builders, a data workbench I use every week, a multi-tenant platform prototype, and a research plan for an open-source editor SDK. None of them has a customer yet. All of them are real, and I'll show any of them live.

Plain facts

  • DevOfCodes LLC is a single-member company. I am the only person who will work on your project.
  • I don't have client case studies yet. I have working software, repositories and process you can read.
  • Falcon, the project manager on this site, is a prototype with no users. It is not for sale.
  • I build mostly with .NET and TypeScript, with Python where the data calls for it.
  • I reply to every message personally, usually within a day.
