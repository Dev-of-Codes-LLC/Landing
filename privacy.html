Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Dev of Codes LLC

In short. This site has no accounts, sets no cookies of its own, and runs no analytics or advertising. The only personal information it collects is what you type into the contact form or send me by email, and that goes to one inbox so I can reply. Your browser also fetches fonts and a script from two third-party services, which see your IP address the way any web request does. Ask and I'll delete what I have about you.

01Who is responsible

Dev of Codes LLC ("I", "me", or "DevOfCodes"), a single-member company based in San Diego, California, operates devofcodes.com and is responsible for the personal information described here. I'm the only person who handles it. Contact details are at the end of this page.

This policy covers the website only. There is no product, app or online service behind it; the one piece of server-side code is the contact-form function described below. An earlier version of this page was a generic template that mentioned user accounts, analytics and advertising; none of those exist on this site, and this version describes what actually happens.

02What is collected, and why

Messages you send me

When you use the contact form you provide your name, your email address, optionally the kind of project you have in mind, and your message. If you email me directly, I receive whatever you include. I use this information for one purpose: to read your message, reply, and, if we go on to work together, to set up and carry out that work. The legal basis, where one is required, is your request (taking steps before entering a contract) and my legitimate interest in answering people who contact me.

Technical information from your browser

Like every website, this one is served over the internet, and the servers involved can record standard technical details of each request: your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser and operating system, and the referring page. I don't add any tracking to this. The hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs for security and operations, and the third-party services listed in section 3 receive the same kind of request details when your browser fetches a resource from them.

Nothing else

The site has no login, no user accounts, no analytics or advertising scripts, no social-media trackers, and no newsletter. It sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser. The live backgrounds are rendered by an open-source library whose optional telemetry is switched off. The contact form includes a hidden field and a timing check to drop automated submissions; neither records anything about you.

04International visitors

I'm in the United States and the services above are operated there, so information you send is processed in the United States. If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another place with its own privacy law, you have the rights described in section 6 regardless, and the legal bases in section 2 apply.

05How long I keep it

Messages are kept for as long as the conversation is live and for a reasonable time afterwards in case you come back, then deleted. If we work together, the records of that work are kept for as long as business and tax rules require. Server logs held by providers follow their own retention periods, which are typically short. If you'd like your messages deleted sooner, ask; see the next section.

06Your rights

You can ask me, at any time and free of charge, to tell you what information I hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to send you a copy, or to stop using it. Email legal@devofcodes.com from the address you originally used, or tell me enough to find your message, and I'll respond within a month. If you believe I've handled your information badly, you can also complain to the privacy regulator where you live.

California residents: I don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California privacy law, and I don't use it for targeted advertising. The rights to know, delete and correct are the ones described above; I won't treat you differently for using them. Because the site does no tracking, it doesn't respond to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals; there is nothing for them to switch off.

07Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and the form submits over HTTPS. Ordinary email is not end-to-end encrypted, so please don't send passwords, keys, financial details or other sensitive information through the form or by email. If we need to exchange something sensitive, I'll arrange a safer channel first.

08Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13 and I don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent me information, let me know and I'll delete it.

09Changes

If the site starts doing something new with personal information, this page will change first and the date at the top will move. Earlier versions are kept in the site's repository history.
